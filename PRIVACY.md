## What this site collects

When you visit, our edge worker records a minimal visit log: your IP address,
the date and time, the page path, your browser's user-agent string, the
referring page, and coarse location derived from your IP (country, region,
city, network/ASN). No cookies are set and no cross-site tracking occurs.

## How it is used and stored

Visit logs are used only to understand aggregate site usage. They may be
forwarded to a spreadsheet operated by the site owner (Google Forms/Sheets,
acting as a processor). Logs are not sold or shared with third parties for
advertising.

## Map data

All map layers are public-record or open datasets (crash records, traffic
counts, parcels with owner information removed, soils, rainfall). See
DATA-ATTRIBUTION.md in the project repository for sources.
