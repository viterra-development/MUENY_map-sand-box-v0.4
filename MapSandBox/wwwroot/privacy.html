Privacy

Last updated: August 2026.

What this site collects

When you visit, our edge server records a minimal visit log: your IP address, the date and time, the page path, your browser's user-agent string, the referring page, and coarse location derived from your IP (country, region, city, network). No advertising cookies are set and no cross-site tracking occurs.

Accounts

If you create an account we store your email address, the name you provide, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), plus any map views you choose to save. A session cookie keeps you signed in; signing out deletes the session.

How data is used

Visit logs are used only to understand aggregate site usage. Account data is used only to operate your account. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Map data

All map layers derive from public-record or open datasets: TxDOT crash records, traffic counts, county appraisal parcels (owner information removed), USDA soils, and NOAA rainfall.
