What MUENY answers

Three questions every city asks — answered on one map.

Where will the next serious crash happen?

Crash-risk scoring over every road segment and intersection, built from TxDOT crash records fused with traffic volumes, road grade, and surface conditions.

  • Risk-ranked segments and intersections
  • Weather and drainage risk factors
  • Defensible inputs for grant applications

What will growth do to our roads?

Parcel-level trip generation for 11 cities — every parcel classified by land use and converted to daily and peak-hour trips on the roads that carry them.

  • ITE-based trip estimates per parcel
  • Road stress index: volume vs. capacity
  • Scenario-ready for development review

Which infrastructure is quietly failing?

Soil permeability, rainfall intensity, and slope layered under the road network — the physical context behind drainage complaints and washouts.

  • SSURGO soils: clay content and permeability
  • NOAA rainfall design intensities
  • Terrain slope at every crash site
