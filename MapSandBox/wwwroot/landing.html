How it works

From public record to first decision in one sitting.

1

We pull the record

TxDOT crash files, traffic counts, county parcels, USDA soils, NOAA rainfall — the data your city already owns but has never seen in one place.

2

The model fuses it

Every road segment scored for crash risk. Every parcel classified and converted to trips. Every drainage complaint matched to its soil and slope.

3

Your staff acts on it

A live map anyone on staff can open, plus briefing reports for council packets and grant applications — signed, sourced, and defensible.

What MUENY answers

Three questions every city asks — answered on one map.

Safety

Where will the next serious crash happen?

Crash-risk scoring over every road segment and intersection, fused from crash records, traffic volumes, road grade, and surface conditions.

  • Risk-ranked segments and intersections
  • Weather and drainage risk factors
  • Defensible inputs for grant applications
Growth

What will growth do to our roads?

Parcel-level trip generation for 11 cities — every parcel classified by land use and converted to daily and peak-hour trips.

  • Trip estimates on every parcel
  • Road stress index: volume vs. capacity
  • Scenario-ready for development review
Ground truth

Which infrastructure is quietly failing?

Soil permeability, rainfall intensity, and slope layered under the road network — the physics behind drainage complaints and washouts.

  • Clay content and permeability, county-wide
  • NOAA rainfall design intensities
  • Terrain slope at every crash site
